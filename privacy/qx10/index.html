QX10 Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · App: QX10 (iOS, bundle ID com.photoprogrammer.QX10Remote)

QX10 (the "App") does not collect or transmit any personal data. The developer operates no servers, and the App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

1. Information we collect

None. The App does not send your name, email, device identifiers, usage data or any other information to the developer or to third parties.

2. Permissions used only on your device

PermissionPurposeSent off the device
Local networkConnects to the Wi-Fi of the camera (Sony DSC-QX10) for live view and shooting control. The camera is the only peer.No
Camera · MicrophoneTakes photos and videos (with sound) with the iPhone camera when no QX10 is connected.No
Photo librarySaves your photos and videos to the library, and accesses it when you delete items taken in the App.No
Location (while using)Only when enabled in Settings, records the shooting location in the photo file.No
Face detectionFor face stickers, Apple's Vision framework finds face positions in the camera image on the device. Face data is never stored or transmitted and is not used to identify anyone.No

3. Information stored on your device

App settings (shooting options, etc.) and the camera Wi-Fi details (network name, password) are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the App. Photos and videos are saved to your photo library, which you control.

4. Sharing and international transfer

None.

5. Children's privacy

The App collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Sony and QX10 are trademarks of Sony Group Corporation. This is an unofficial app not affiliated with Sony.
